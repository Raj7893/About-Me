My Unity Game

One of my achievements so far is completing a game in unity game engine. This project uses my passion for coding and turns it into an experience. Through this project I just did not improve my skills in Coding. I also learned about how to document my work properly. I learned that when universities or job employers look at your game project they also look at if you documented how you made the game. If you document everything you can look back at your project and reflect on it. Reflection is very important in any kind of project because you can look at your mistakes and improve them. My goal in Unity game development is to make a game with a complex NPC system. An NPC stands for Non-Player Character. In games, it refers to any character that is controlled by the game's computer programming or AI rather than by a human player.

Robotics

Beyond game development, my passion is robotics. Robotics is one of my favorites because it incorporates a lot of skills I have. In robots you have to find a problem and create a solution around it. Before you make a robot you have to follow the design process. First you have to ask what your robot needs to do, second you need to design an idea on how you are going to fix that problem, third you have to make the robot, fourth you have to test the robot and finally you improve your robot using your testing results. Feedback & Growth

I constantly take feedback from my teachers and mentors. Taking Feedback is a very important part of growth. When you take feedback from your seniors you learn from their experiences and the mistakes they made to get to the point that they are at. Feedback can help you improve your projects and elevate the quality of your project. Last year in robotics 9 I did not use the design process properly because I thought “What is the point of this “ then my peer touter and my teacher gave me feedback and explained to me the importance of the design process.Another example where i took feedback was when i made my game i showed it to my mentor and my mentor told me the importance of documentation and how employers and universities look at documentation along with your game.
